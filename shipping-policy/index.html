Delivery Attempts

Our courier partner makes multiple delivery attempts. If all attempts are unsuccessful:

  • The package may be held at the nearest courier hub for pickup
  • You'll be notified via SMS/email with hub details
  • Unclaimed packages may be returned to us after the holding period
