Sugar Honesty

Yes, there's 6.9g of glucose. Here's exactly why.

Your gut absorbs sodium and water through a pathway called the sodium-glucose cotransporter. No glucose, no cotransport — slower absorption. Same mechanism that makes ORS work when you're sick.
